Reviewer Guidelines 📌

1. Review Timeline ⏰

Reviewers are expected to complete their reviews within 2 weeks of accepting the review request. If you require additional time, please contact the editorial office as soon as possible.

2. Ethical Guidelines 📄

  • AI Usage Policy: Reviewers are permitted to use AI tools to assist in understanding the manuscript (e.g., summarizing, explaining concepts). However, reviewers are strictly prohibited from using AI tools to generate review comments or make final decisions. All reviews must be written by the reviewers themselves based on their own expertise and critical analysis.
  • Maintain confidentiality of the manuscript and review process.
  • Decline to review if you have a conflict of interest with any author.
  • Provide objective, fair, and constructive feedback.

3. Reviewer Responsibilities

  • Accept review assignments only if you have sufficient expertise and time
  • Complete reviews within the agreed timeframe
  • Provide thorough and constructive feedback
  • Maintain professionalism and respect in all communications
  • Update your reviewer profile regularly

4. Review Content Recommendations 💡

We encourage reviewers to provide comprehensive and actionable feedback. A high-quality review typically includes:

  • Structure: We recommend including at least three strengths (pros) and three constructive suggestions (cons/suggestions) in your review.
  • Evaluation Criteria: Please consider the following aspects when evaluating the manuscript:
    • Completeness: Is the work comprehensive? Are there any missing experiments or analyses?
    • Novelty: Does the method or approach offer significant innovation compared to existing literature?
    • Reproducibility: Are the methods and results described in sufficient detail to allow reproduction?
