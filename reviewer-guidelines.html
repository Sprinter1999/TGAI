Reviewer Guidelines 📌

1. Review Timeline ⏰

Reviewers are expected to complete their reviews within 2 weeks of accepting the review request. If you require additional time, please contact the editorial office as soon as possible.

2. Ethical Guidelines 📄

  • No AI Usage: Reviewers are not permitted to use AI tools for the review process. All reviews must be based on your own expertise and critical analysis.
  • Maintain confidentiality of the manuscript and review process.
  • Decline to review if you have a conflict of interest with any author.
  • Provide objective, fair, and constructive feedback.

3. Reviewer Responsibilities

  • Accept review assignments only if you have sufficient expertise and time
  • Complete reviews within the agreed timeframe
  • Provide thorough and constructive feedback
  • Maintain professionalism and respect in all communications
  • Update your reviewer profile regularly
