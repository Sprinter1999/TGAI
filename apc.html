Article Processing Charge (APC) 💰

To support Open Access publishing, TGAI charges an Article Processing Charge (APC) to cover the costs of peer review, copyediting, typesetting, long-term archiving, and journal management.

Fees

Standard APC: $500 USD per accepted manuscript.

Submission Fee: None. It is free to submit your manuscript for review.

Waivers

We offer fee waivers or discounts for authors from low-income countries or those with limited research funding. Please contact the editorial office for more information.

Revenue Sources

TGAI's revenue comes from article processing charges (APCs), institutional subscriptions, and grants from research organizations. Publication fees do not influence editorial decisions.
