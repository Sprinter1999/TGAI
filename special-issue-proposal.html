Special Issue Proposal 💡

TGAI welcomes proposals for Special Issues that focus on specific, timely topics within the scope of the journal.

Proposal Requirements

Proposals should include:

  • Title of the Special Issue
  • Names and affiliations of Guest Editors
  • A brief description of the topic and its importance
  • A list of potential topics/themes
  • Proposed timeline (submission deadline, review process, publication date)

Please submit your proposal to editorial@tgai-journal.org.
