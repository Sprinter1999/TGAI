Trends in Generative AI (TGAI) 🤖

Welcome to the official website of Trends in Generative AI (TGAI). We aim to publish high-quality research papers in all areas of generative artificial intelligence, including large language models, diffusion models, generative adversarial networks, and more.

About Our Journal 📚

TGAI is a peer-reviewed open-access journal that provides a platform for researchers and practitioners to share their innovative research findings and technical contributions in generative AI. Our editorial board consists of leading experts from around the world to ensure the quality and impact of published papers.
