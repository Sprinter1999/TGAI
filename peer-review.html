Peer Review Process 🔍

All submissions to TGAI undergo rigorous double-blind peer review by at least two independent experts in the field.

Process Overview

  1. Initial Screening: The Editor-in-Chief checks the manuscript for suitability, formatting, and plagiarism.
  2. Assignment: The manuscript is assigned to an Associate Editor based on the topic.
  3. External Review: The Associate Editor invites at least two independent reviewers.
  4. Review: Reviewers assess the manuscript and provide comments (typically 2-4 weeks).
  5. Decision: The Associate Editor recommends a decision (Accept, Minor Revision, Major Revision, Reject) to the Editor-in-Chief.
  6. Final Decision: The Editor-in-Chief makes the final decision and notifies the authors.

Review Timeline: Typically 3-4 weeks, subject to reviewer availability.
